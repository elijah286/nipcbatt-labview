 Switches Characteristics[Switch Characteristics]

Contains the maximum rating for voltage and current for mux and shunt configurations.

    		MUX Characteristics[Mux Characteristics]

    Returns the maximum rating of switching characteristics for the module.

    Switching voltage refers to the maximum signal voltage that the switch module can safely maintain.

    Switching current is the maximum rated current that can flow through the switch as it makes or breaks a contact. Switching active currents results in arcing that can damage the contacts of electromechanical relays.

    Max. DC Voltage (V) - Maximum DC voltage the module can support.

    Max. Switching DC Current (A) - Maximum DC Current the module can support when switching.

    Max. AC Voltage (V) - Maximum AC voltage the module can support.

    Max. Switching AC Current (A) - Maximum AC Current the module can support when switching.
