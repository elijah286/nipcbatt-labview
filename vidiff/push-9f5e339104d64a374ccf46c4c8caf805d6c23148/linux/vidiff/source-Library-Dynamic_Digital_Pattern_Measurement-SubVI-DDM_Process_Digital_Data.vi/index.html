DAQ_Dynamic Digital Pattern Measurement.lvlib:DDM Process Digital Data.vi

Processes the captured 1D Digital waveform data where digital lines are presented in each array elements into a single Digital waveform format where all digital lines data are appended in consecutive MSB bits.
